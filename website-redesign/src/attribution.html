Make it yours.
Credit where it began.

VineMix welcomes forks and modified versions. Add features, remove things, change the design or give your version a new name. Please keep the original project's contribution identifiable.

A short credit is enough.

Please include this acknowledgement in your About, Credits or Licences screen, with a link to the original project:

Based on VineMix by acoyeen.

You can link to vinemix.app or the original GitHub repository. For software without a graphical interface, help text or accompanying documentation is a suitable place.

You do not need to keep the logo, call your program VineMix or display a permanent badge. Please identify your version as an independent fork and avoid suggesting that the VineMix maintainer produced or endorsed it.

What the licence requires.

VineMix is MIT-licensed. When distributing copies or substantial portions of its code, preserve the copyright and permission notice supplied with the version you used. Include the complete MIT licence, including its warranty disclaimer, with source and binary distributions.

The current copyright notice is Copyright (c) 2026 acoyeen (VineMix, https://vinemix.app). A credit sentence or link alone does not replace the full licence notice. Third-party components and optional drivers retain their own terms.

Required notices. Requested visibility.

The in-program credit above is our request, not an extra MIT condition. MIT permits its required notices to accompany a program in licence files; a missing About-screen credit alone does not establish a licence violation.

Earlier releases keep their original notices and permissions. This update does not retroactively add requirements to those versions, though we welcome the same visible credit from their forks.

Read the complete attribution policy on GitHub for details and guidance on missing credit, or visit our third-party credits.
