# VineMix 0.6.1

In **Settings**, select **Audio device visibility** to expand its three filters in place. They appear together in a shaded group. Search for a filter to reveal it, or use the arrow keys to expand and collapse the group.

Hovering over the minimap now leaves the graph unobstructed. Click or drag the minimap to navigate as before.

Choose **Settings → Check for updates** or download **VineMix-0.6.1-Setup-x64.exe** or **VineMix-0.6.1-Standalone-x64.exe** from the [release page](https://github.com/acoyeen/vine-mix/releases/tag/v0.6.1). Use the package that matches your installation. Save your work before installing. Audio pauses briefly during restart.

If a 0.5 installation cancels its in-app update, follow the [manual upgrade instructions](../INSTALLATION_AND_UPDATES.md). Each download is accompanied by checksums and signed update metadata.
