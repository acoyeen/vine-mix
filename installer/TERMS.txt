YOUR FILES AND SETTINGS

Application files are installed in the folder shown by Setup. By default this is
%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\VineMix. Your saved workspace, preferences, backups and
logs remain in %LOCALAPPDATA%\VineMix, separately from application files.
Existing saved settings are retained. Uninstalling VineMix retains these files.

A copy already running from another folder shares the same settings and audio
engine identity. Setup does not stop that copy or launch another one alongside
it. Quit the existing copy from its tray menu before using the installed copy.

PRIVACY AND AUDIO

Setup checks installed audio drivers and running VineMix processes locally.
The included driver packages are available offline. Opening a vendor website
uses your browser. Setup does not upload your audio, settings or saved workspaces.
VineMix's routing and audio settings remain under your control. Review the user
guide and test routing and levels before relying on it for important work.
