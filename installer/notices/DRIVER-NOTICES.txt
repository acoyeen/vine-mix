VineMix: optional virtual audio drivers
Reviewed 21 October 2026

VineMix is a separate application. The two optional drivers included with this
free, non-commercial installer belong to their respective vendors and keep
their own licences. VineMix's MIT licence does not relicense these drivers.
All original package files, documentation and vendor terms are preserved.

VB-CABLE (base cable), by VB-Audio Software
Origin: https://www.vb-cable.com/
Product and download: https://vb-audio.com/Cable/
Licensing and contribution information: https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm

VB-CABLE is donationware; all contributions are welcome. Please support
VB-Audio if you find it useful. Professional use and deployment have additional
licensing conditions. Read the original readme.txt in the VB-CABLE package and
the vendor's current licensing page before installing or deploying it.

VB-CABLE carries sound from other applications into VineMix. This installer
includes only the base cable, not the separately obtained A+B or C+D products.

Virtual Audio Cable Lite 4.71, by Eugene Muzychenko
Product and download: https://vac.muzychenko.net/en/download.htm
Licence: https://vac.muzychenko.net/en/license.txt
Distribution policy: https://vac.muzychenko.net/en/manual/distrib.htm

VAC Lite is restricted to private home use unrelated to income generation.
The complete, unchanged Lite package is included for free, non-commercial
distribution. It must not be sold on its own or within another product.
Read its original license.txt before installing. This is the Lite edition,
not the Trial or paid edition. The vendor's terms govern your use.

VAC Lite carries VineMix's mixed output into another application as a
recording device or microphone.

Driver installation is optional and uses the vendors' original installers.
Each vendor's terms must be accepted separately. Existing compatible drivers
should be retained; do not reinstall, replace, remove or rename them merely
to install VineMix. Driver installation may require administrator approval
and a Windows restart. Uninstalling VineMix should leave shared drivers in
place so other applications can continue using them.
