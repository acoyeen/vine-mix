# Crediting VineMix

Updated 28 October 2026.

A readable summary is available at [vinemix.app/attribution](https://vinemix.app/attribution).

VineMix welcomes forks, modifications and new versions. You may add, remove or
change features, use your own product name and release your work under the
permissions in the [MIT licence](LICENSE). Please keep the original project's
contribution identifiable when sharing a version based on VineMix.

## Required licence notices

When distributing copies or substantial portions of VineMix, preserve the full
copyright and permission notice from the version you used. For the current
source, the copyright notice identifies both the maintainer and the project:

```text
Copyright (c) 2026 acoyeen (VineMix, https://vinemix.app)
```

Include the complete [LICENSE](LICENSE), including its warranty disclaimer,
with source and binary distributions. A credit sentence or website link alone
does not replace the licence notice. You may add accurate notices for your own
contributions alongside the original notice. Preserve applicable third-party
notices too; see [distribution guidance](docs/DISTRIBUTION.md).

## Requested credit inside modified programs

Please include a readable acknowledgement in your program's About, Credits or
Licences screen, with a link to the original website or repository. One short
line is enough:

> Based on VineMix by acoyeen — https://vinemix.app

Alternatively, link **VineMix** to
[the original repository](https://github.com/acoyeen/vine-mix). For software
without a graphical interface, a help command or accompanying documentation is
a suitable place. We also welcome this credit in your README and download page.

You do not need to keep our logo, use VineMix as your product's name, show a
splash screen or keep the credit permanently visible. Please describe modified
versions as independent forks and avoid implying that the VineMix maintainer
produced or endorsed them. Identifying VineMix as the source of your work is
welcome.

The in-program placement and linking above are the maintainer's attribution
request, not additional MIT licence conditions. MIT requires preservation of
the notices, but permits them to accompany the program in licence files. This
policy does not turn an otherwise compliant distribution into a violation
solely because its interface lacks an acknowledgement.

## Scope and existing versions

VineMix remains MIT-licensed. This document adds no fee, non-commercial
restriction, source-sharing obligation or requirement to seek permission for a
fork. Dependencies and bundled drivers retain their separate terms.

The expanded copyright notice applies to this source revision and subsequent
copies carrying it. Earlier releases retain the notices and permissions they
were supplied with; this update does not retroactively require their users to
add the new project name or link. We request the same visible acknowledgement
from forks of those releases.

## If attribution appears to be missing

First identify the VineMix version and material used, and check the fork's
source, accompanying licence files and in-program notices. An absent About
credit alone does not establish a licence violation. Keep dated links and a
copy of the relevant distribution before contacting its maintainer.

If the required copyright or permission notice is missing, point to the MIT
condition and ask for the complete notice to be restored in the source and
downloads. If the notice is present but difficult to find, point to this policy
and request the short in-program acknowledgement above. Escalation should be
based on the actual missing requirement and evidence, with legal advice for a
disputed infringement claim.

## Basis for this approach

Primary sources checked on 28 October 2026:

- [MIT licence, Open Source Initiative](https://opensource.org/license/mit):
  permits broad reuse subject to retaining the copyright and permission notice.
  It does not specify an About screen or a mandatory hyperlink.
- [Apache License 2.0, section 4](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0):
  has explicit attribution and NOTICE provisions, but also allows notices in
  accompanying files or documentation. Switching would not by itself ensure
  in-program credit.

Project decision: retain MIT, identify VineMix and its website directly in the
copyright notice, and make the requested visible acknowledgement explicit.
This preserves the project's existing reuse permissions. A mandatory interface
credit would require a separate licensing decision and review; this document
does not claim to impose one or guarantee enforcement in every jurisdiction.
